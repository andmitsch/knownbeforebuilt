About the imprint

Grant & Bear Publishing is the imprint under which Andreas Mitschke publishes his work. It was founded in 2026 with Known Before Built, and it exists for books written by practitioners for practitioners: useful first, carefully made, and built to be kept on the desk rather than the shelf.
